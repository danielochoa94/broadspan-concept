Hülya Sögüt

Partner

Mrs. Sögüt has been a sustainability expert for over 18 years, with 10 years of deal experience in LAC. She leads ESG and impact management incl. actively managing portfolio companies’ performance, as well as investor relations, for BSAM’s private capital strategy in LAC.

Prior to joining BSAM, she was a Partner with Portland Private Equity, a middle-market private equity firm focused on the Caribbean, Central America, and Colombia. Mrs. Sögüt pioneered a groundbreaking ESG and impact program and co-led the successful fundraising of the successor fund. Additionally, Mrs. Sögüt has been consulting many years for DEG and the World Bank working on worldwide investments in projects, companies, banks, and funds. Prior to her investment work, she held roles at PwC in sustainability advisory and audit of development cooperation projects.

Mrs. Sögüt is fluent in five languages, and holds an MA in International Politics as well as a Diploma in International Business Administration.

 

Spoken languages: English, Spanish, German, French, Turkish.
