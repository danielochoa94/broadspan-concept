Who we are

We are a leading independent investment banking firm
that specializes in Latin America and the Caribbean.

Founded in 2001, BroadSpan provides corporations, partnerships and government institutions with impartial advice related to cross border mergers, acquisitions, financial restructurings and capital raising in Latin America and the Caribbean. BroadSpan delivers solutions to clients from its offices in Miami, Rio de Janeiro, Sao Paulo and Bogotá and through affiliate offices located in 30 countries around the world.
