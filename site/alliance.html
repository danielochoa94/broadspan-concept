Mergers Alliance

BroadSpan is a member of Mergers Alliance, a partnership of independent corporate finance firms across the world’s major economies.

Members share buyer networks, deal flow and sector insight. A client in Medellín or São Paulo gets senior local coverage in Frankfurt, Toronto or Tokyo, and partners abroad bring their clients to Latin America through us.
