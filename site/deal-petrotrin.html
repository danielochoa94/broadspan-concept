BroadSpan Advises Bondholders on Trinidad Petroleum Holdings Exchange Offer

Client
TPHL Bondholder Committee
Country
Trinidad & Tobago
Sector
Oil & gas
Role
Exclusive financial advisor
Value
US$570.3 million

June 2019. BroadSpan Capital acted as exclusive financial advisor to the bondholder committee of Trinidad Petroleum Holdings Limited (“TPHL”) in the exchange offer for its bonds due 2019 and 2022.

The committee, formed by large international investment firms, and other significant holders together held US$273.9 million of the bonds and entered into an exchange support agreement with TPHL to participate in its amended exchange offer. With the US$152.6 million already tendered, support reached US$426.5 million.

The holders’ decision followed favorable changes to commercial terms, disclosure of TPHL’s conditional term loan commitments, and extensive discussions on the legal review of the transaction structure, inter-creditor equity and shareholder support. Arnold & Porter Kaye Scholer LLP acted as the committee’s legal advisor.
