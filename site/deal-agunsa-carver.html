BroadSpan Advises AGUNSA on Acquisition of Carver Maritime Manatee

Client
AGUNSA
Country
United States
Sector
Logistics
Role
Exclusive financial advisor

Miami, Florida, February 2023. BroadSpan Capital acted as exclusive financial advisor to Agencias Universales S.A. (“AGUNSA”) and its shareholders on the acquisition of 75% of the outstanding shares of Carver Maritime Manatee (“CMM”).

The acquisition strengthens AGUNSA’s position as a global leader in shipping and logistics across port operations, freight forwarding and transportation. CMM’s dry bulk facilities serve the Tampa Bay and Manatee area, and AGUNSA’s global network should bring immediate trade synergies on Florida’s west coast, a popular entry point for South and Central American exporters shipping to the United States.

AGUNSA, headquartered in Santiago, Chile, is a leading multinational shipping and logistics company with operations in more than 20 countries across Latin America, the Caribbean, Europe, Asia and the United States.

CMM, a port-logistics subsidiary of Tampa-based Carver Companies, transloads bulk cargo through its facilities and operates an important deep-water port for ships accessing the Gulf of Mexico and Florida’s west coast.
