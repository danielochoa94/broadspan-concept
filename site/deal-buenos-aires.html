BroadSpan Advises Bondholders in Province of Buenos Aires Debt Restructuring

Client
Bondholder Steering Committee
Country
Argentina
Sector
Sub-sovereign
Role
Co-financial advisor
Value
US$7.2 billion

September 2021. BroadSpan Capital acted as financial advisor, alongside Mens Sana Advisors, to the bondholder steering committee in the restructuring of the Province of Buenos Aires’ external debt.

In February 2019, the steering committee and other significant holders supported the Province’s amended consent solicitation on its 10.875% bonds due 2021, viewing the revised commercial terms as an indication that the Province intended to negotiate a comprehensive restructuring in good faith. Arnold & Porter Kaye Scholer LLP and Bruchou, Fernández Madero & Lombardi acted as the committee’s legal advisors.

In April 2020, with the Province facing debt service challenges compounded by COVID-19, the committee, representing a group holding over 40% of the Province’s foreign-law bonds, rejected a unilateral restructuring proposal launched without engaging on the cash flow relief structures it had outlined, and called for good faith negotiations grounded in substantiated economic and financial information.
