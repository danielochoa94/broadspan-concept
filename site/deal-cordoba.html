BroadSpan Advises Bondholders in Argentina’s Province of Córdoba Restructuring

Client
Ad Hoc Bondholder Group
Country
Argentina
Sector
Sub-sovereign
Role
Exclusive financial advisor
Value
US$1.669 billion

February 2021. BroadSpan Capital acted as exclusive financial advisor to the Ad Hoc Bondholder Group formed to address the restructuring of the Province of Córdoba’s US$1.669 billion of international bonds due 2021, 2024 and 2027.

In September 2020, the Province opened discussions with bondholders, citing Argentina’s macroeconomic situation, limited access to capital markets and disruption from COVID-19. BroadSpan supported the formation of the Group, institutional asset managers holding over 50% of the bonds, which acknowledged the pandemic’s impact while holding that any restructuring had to reflect the Province’s true repayment capacity and respect the bonds’ contractual terms.

Over five months of negotiations, the Province’s original proposal was amended five times. A consensual agreement was reached on January 11, 2021, and the Province received consents of over 96%, triggering the bonds’ collective action clauses. Settlement occurred on January 26, 2021. White & Case LLP acted as the Group’s legal advisor.

The Group included funds managed or advised by Ayres Investment Management, BFAM Partners, Fidelity Management & Research, GML Capital, Redwood Capital Management, VR Advisory Services, Wellington Management and Western Asset Management.
