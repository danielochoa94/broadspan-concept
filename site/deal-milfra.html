BroadSpan Advises Brazil’s Milfra on Sale to France’s Nemera

Client
Milfra
Country
Brazil
Sector
Plastic packaging
Role
Exclusive financial advisor

Rio de Janeiro, Brazil, June 2021. BroadSpan Capital acted as exclusive financial advisor to Milfra and its shareholders in its sale to Nemera.

The acquisition expands Nemera’s footprint in Latin America, extends its product offering and lays a foundation for contract manufacturing. With a key applicator pure player on board, Nemera can now address specific patient and customer needs in gynecology and urology in Brazil.

Milfra is a leading Brazilian manufacturer of plastic products for the pharmaceutical industry, specializing in personal care applicators and oral dosers. Family-owned since its founding in 1966, it operates a state-of-the-art plant in Jaguariúna, São Paulo state, with over 130 employees.

Based in La Verpillière, France, Nemera is a world-leading provider of drug delivery devices, from medicine droppers and ointment applicators to electronic devices, with 2020 revenues of €430 million and five plants in France, the United States, Germany and Poland.
