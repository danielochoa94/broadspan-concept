BroadSpan Advises Bancolombia on Restructuring of Abengoa Peru Debt

Client
Bancolombia
Country
Peru
Sector
Energy
Role
Exclusive financial advisor

Medellín, Colombia, February 2025. BroadSpan Capital acted as exclusive financial advisor to Bancolombia S.A. in the settlement of the outstanding financial obligations of ATN3 S.A., a subsidiary of Abengoa Peru.

The transaction enabled Bancolombia to eliminate its exposure to ATN3, a 332km electricity transmission project in the Cusco region of Peru, while allowing Spain's Acciona Concesiones to acquire ATN3 and take control of the development of a crucial energy transmission project.

“We are very pleased to have been able to successfully support Bancolombia in what was a complex situation involving not only restructuring negotiations but also coordination with Acciona's acquisition process.”

Mike Gerrard, CEO, BroadSpan Capital

Bancolombia, headquartered in Medellín, operates as a full-service financial institution serving nearly 30 million individual and corporate customers, primarily in Colombia and Central America.
