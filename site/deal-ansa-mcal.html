BroadSpan Advises ANSA McAL on Acquisition of Bank of Baroda Trinidad

Client
ANSA McAL
Country
Trinidad & Tobago
Sector
Banking
Role
Financial advisor

March 2021. BroadSpan Capital acted as financial advisor to ANSA McAL, a Caribbean conglomerate, on its acquisition of 100% of Bank of Baroda (Trinidad and Tobago) Limited.

BroadSpan executed the transaction jointly with its Indian partner, Singhi Advisors, giving ANSA McAL the cross-border resources to complete a complex transaction. After working with ANSA McAL for several months on an acquisition search, BroadSpan identified the target and navigated a complicated negotiation and regulatory process involving stakeholders in Trinidad and India.

Through the acquisition, ANSA Merchant Bank, a subsidiary of ANSA McAL, extends its offering into commercial and retail banking from its focus on investment banking and investment management. ANSA McAL is listed on the Trinidad and Tobago Stock Exchange, with a last reported market capitalization of US$1.44 billion.

Bank of Baroda, the Indian government-owned bank, began operating in Trinidad and Tobago in October 2007 and is one of only eight banks in the country, lending to retail and corporate clients through branches in Port of Spain, San Fernando and Chaguanas.
