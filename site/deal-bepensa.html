BroadSpan Advises Bepensa on Acquisition of Crédito Real USA Finance

Client
Bepensa
Country
United States
Sector
Financial services
Role
Exclusive financial advisor

Fort Lauderdale, Florida, April 2023. BroadSpan Capital acted as exclusive financial advisor to Bepensa, S.A. de C.V. (“Bepensa”) in the acquisition of 97.6% of the outstanding shares of Crédito Real USA Finance (“Crusafin”) from Crédito Real SAB de CV SOFOM ENR (“Crédito Real”).

The transaction was carried out under Section 363 of the U.S. Bankruptcy Code as part of Crédito Real’s court-mandated judicial liquidation in Mexico. Bepensa secured a “stalking horse” position, and its bid was confirmed by the U.S. Bankruptcy Court for the District of Delaware, which oversaw the process.

“We are very pleased to have successfully advised Bepensa in what was a complex process that leveraged BroadSpan’s cross-border M&A capabilities as well as our debt restructuring and U.S. Chapter 11 experience.”

Mike Gerrard, CEO, BroadSpan Capital

Founded in 1946, Bepensa is a Mexican multinational group of more than 40 companies in the beverage, industrial, automotive, spirits and financial sectors. Crusafin, a U.S. specialty finance platform focused on subprime auto lending across most major U.S. markets, expands Bepensa’s footprint in the United States. With a captive loan portfolio of over US$159 million, it is expected to bring immediate synergies with Bepensa’s financial services platform.
