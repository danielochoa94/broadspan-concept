BroadSpan Advises Grupo Boa Praça on Sale of Ship Supply Operations to Wrist Ship Supply

Client
Grupo Boa Praça
Country
Brazil
Sector
Maritime supply
Role
Exclusive financial advisor

Rio de Janeiro, Brazil, December 2024. BroadSpan Capital acted as exclusive financial advisor to Grupo Boa Praça and its shareholders in the sale of its ship supply operations to Wrist Ship Supply.

The acquisition extends Wrist’s commercial and operational presence to a new continent, reinforcing its commitment to the continuous expansion of its global maritime services and supply network.

“We are very happy to have supported Boa Praça in the successful completion of what was a complex carve-out transaction that leveraged BroadSpan’s technical experience as well as our international reach in identifying the optimal buyer.”

Leonardo Antunes, Head of Brazil, BroadSpan Capital

Boa Praça General Ship Supplier, headquartered in Vitória, Espírito Santo, is Brazil’s leader in ship supplies, from food, beverages and cleaning materials to safety equipment, lubricants and deck equipment. It serves 25 Brazilian ports from four bases in Rio de Janeiro, Macaé, Vitória and Recife, with 11,500 m² of storage and approximately 3,000 deliveries a year.

Wrist Ship Supply, based in Aalborg, Denmark, is the global leader in maritime provisions and supplies for vessels and offshore platforms, with approximately 12% market share, more than 30 locations and over 1,800 employees.
