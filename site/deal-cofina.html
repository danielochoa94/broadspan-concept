BroadSpan Advises Bondholders in Puerto Rico COFINA Restructuring

Client
COFINA bondholder group
Country
Puerto Rico
Sector
Public sector
Role
Financial advisor

April 16, 2019. BroadSpan Capital acted as financial advisor to an institutional bondholder group in the restructuring of the debt of the Puerto Rico Sales Tax Financing Corporation (“COFINA”). The group controlled approximately US$1.9 billion of COFINA Senior and Junior bonds prior to the restructuring.

The restructuring was undertaken under the Puerto Rico Oversight, Management and Economic Stability Act (“PROMESA”), whose Financial Oversight and Management Board filed for relief on behalf of the Commonwealth and commenced debt adjustment cases under Title III, allowing debt terms to be adjusted using certain sections of the U.S. Bankruptcy Code.

Over twenty-two months, BroadSpan and the group negotiated with the Oversight Board, the Commonwealth, their advisors and other holders of Puerto Rico debt, balancing settlement discussions while building consensus for a fair and transparent process. Once the Oversight Board, the Commonwealth and a majority of COFINA holders reached agreement, the U.S. District Court for the District of Puerto Rico approved a Plan of Adjustment in February 2019, opening the exchange of existing COFINA bonds for new bonds.

Senior COFINA holders received new bonds and cash equal to approximately 93% of their claims, and Junior holders received new bonds equal to approximately 56%. Subject to certain conditions, participating holders also shared pro rata in cash equal to 2% of aggregate claims, compensating for the cost of negotiation, confirmation and consummation.
