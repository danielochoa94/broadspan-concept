Private Capital

Flexible Capital. Tailored Partnerships. Built for Growth.

Growth capital for middle-market companies in Central America, the Caribbean and Colombia.

Overview

The region is home to a broad universe of well-run middle-market businesses with strong fundamentals and limited access to long-term, flexible growth funding.

Bank credit is short-term and collateral-driven, and larger regional funds rarely invest at the scale these companies need.

BSAM’s private capital strategy fills that gap.

We invest selectively in companies positioned for scalable expansion, partnering with entrepreneurs, families, corporate groups and fellow institutional investors, and combining regional specialization, disciplined structuring and active engagement.

What We Look For

  • Defensible competitive positions

  • Capable management teams, open to institutional governance

  • Clear, fundable expansion pathways

  • Potential to institutionalize and attract strategic buyers or the next round of capital

Flexible Structures

We structure each transaction around the company’s cash flows and growth plan: aligning incentives, optimizing the capital structure, protecting against downside and building exit visibility from inception, while allowing existing shareholders to retain control.

  1. Common equity
  2. Preferred and structured preferred equity
  3. Mezzanine instruments with equity participation, on a selective basis
