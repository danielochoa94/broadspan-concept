BroadSpan Advises AGS on the Acquisition of A.R. Savage & Son

Client
AGS
Country
United States
Sector
Logistics
Role
Exclusive financial advisor

Miami, Florida, February 2024. BroadSpan Capital acted as exclusive financial advisor to Agencias Universales S.A. (“AGS”) and its shareholders on the acquisition of 70% of the outstanding shares of A.R. Savage & Son (“A.R. Savage”).

The acquisition supports AGS’s global expansion and strengthens its agency business. As one of only 34 Certified Agent Members of the Association of Ship Brokers and Agents in the United States, A.R. Savage gives AGS a platform for growth from Tampa, with immediate commercial synergies in the Gulf Coast, the preferred U.S. port of entry for South and Central American companies.

“We congratulate AGS on their continued successful expansion into the U.S. market and are very pleased to leverage our regional and U.S. capabilities to support in this effort.”

Mike Gerrard, CEO, BroadSpan Capital

AGS is part of AGUNSA, a leading multinational shipping and logistics company with operations in more than 20 countries across Latin America, the Caribbean, Europe, Asia and the United States.

With over 70 years in operation, A.R. Savage is a leader in the U.S. maritime community, providing ship agency, freight forwarding and line handling services on Florida’s west coast and abroad.
