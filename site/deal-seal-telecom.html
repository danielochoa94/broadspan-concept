BroadSpan Advises Brazil’s Seal Telecom on Sale to Convergint Technologies

Client
Seal Telecom
Country
Brazil
Sector
Technology
Role
Exclusive financial advisor

Rio de Janeiro, Brazil, April 2021. BroadSpan Capital acted as exclusive financial advisor to Seal Telecom and its shareholders in its sale to Convergint Technologies.

The acquisition marks Convergint’s expansion across Latin America and strengthens its ability to serve multinational companies and deliver technology solutions in the region and worldwide.

Seal Telecom is a Brazilian multinational integrator of audio and video, unified communication, smart building, broadcast, and fire and security solutions, with over 500 employees and offices in Brazil, Argentina, Chile, Peru, Colombia, Costa Rica and Mexico.

Convergint Technologies is a US$1.4 billion global systems integrator that designs, installs and services electronic security, cybersecurity, fire alarm and life safety systems, with over 5,000 employees and more than 150 locations across North America, Europe and Asia Pacific.
