BroadSpan Advises Bondholders in Cap Cana Debt Restructuring

Client
Cap Cana Bondholders Committee
Country
Dominican Republic
Sector
Real estate
Role
Exclusive financial advisor

August 28, 2017. BroadSpan Capital acted as exclusive financial advisor to the bondholder committee formed to address the restructuring of Cap Cana, S.A.’s 10% Senior Secured Notes due 2016 and 10% Senior Secured Recovery Notes due 2016.

In November 2011, Cap Cana defaulted on the notes after missing an October 31 interest payment, stating that its projected cash flows would not allow it to resume interest payments for the foreseeable future. BroadSpan coordinated the formation of the committee, made up of institutional asset managers holding a majority of the notes plus 11 international and regional ad hoc members with additional exposure.

Together with the committee and its legal advisor, Greenberg Traurig LLP, BroadSpan led several years of negotiations with Cap Cana and its advisors, culminating in an Offer to Exchange in March 2017. The structure gave bondholders greater control and visibility over the monetization of their collective investment while supporting the reorganization and relaunch of the Cap Cana project in Punta Cana.

When the offer expired on July 13, 2017, participation reached 98.7% for the Senior Notes and 98.5% for the Recovery Notes. The new 10% Senior Secured Notes due 2024, issued by Caribbean Debt Recovery, Ltd., have an aggregate face value of US$212.0 million.
