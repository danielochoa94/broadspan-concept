BroadSpan Advises FMO and DEG in Costa Rica Restructuring

Client
FMO & DEG
Country
Costa Rica
Sector

Role
Exclusive financial advisor

San José, Costa Rica, June 2020. BroadSpan Capital acted as exclusive financial advisor to Nederlandse Financierings-Maatschappij voor Ontwikkelingslanden N.V. (“FMO”) and Deutsche Investitions- und Entwicklungsgesellschaft mbH (“DEG”) in the financial restructuring and successful resolution of their exposure to Upala Agrícola.

Upala Agrícola, an agro-industrial company in Upala, Alajuela, in northern Costa Rica, is one of the world’s premier pineapple producers, with nearly 2,000 hectares dedicated to the production and export of fresh pineapple.

FMO and DEG, the Dutch and German development banks, provide long-term capital to traditionally under-served countries and industries. Dentons Muñoz acted as local legal advisor to FMO and DEG.
