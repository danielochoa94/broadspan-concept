BroadSpan Advises AGS on the Acquisition of Omni Transloading

Client
AGS
Country
United States
Sector

Role
Exclusive financial advisor

Miami, Florida, February 2024. BroadSpan Capital acted as exclusive financial advisor to Agencias Universales S.A. (“AGS”) and its shareholders on the acquisition of 55% of the outstanding shares of Omni Transloading & Logistics and Omni Bulk Services, Inc. (“Omni”).

The acquisition amplifies AGS’s international reach and fortifies its intermodal transportation business, while Omni’s rapidly expanding platform offers synergies with AGS’s existing operations in South Florida.

“Very pleased to support AGS in yet another successful acquisition in the United States while leveraging our logistics sector capabilities.”

Mike Gerrard, CEO, BroadSpan Capital

AGS is a leading multinational shipping and logistics company with operations in more than 20 countries across Latin America, the Caribbean, Europe, Asia and the United States.

Omni has been a premier rail transloader in South Florida since 2014, offering warehousing, handling and trucking from centrally located rail and non-rail facilities, along with supply-chain management and cross-dock distribution.
