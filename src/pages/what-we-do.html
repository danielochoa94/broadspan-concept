Mergers & Acquisitions

BroadSpan has a proven track record of successful M&A advisory transactions across a range of countries and industries where we have acted on the sell-side, the buy-side and on merger transactions.

Sell-Side Advisory

We seek to maximize a company’s value by executing a full or partial sale to a third party, efficiently meeting the objectives of shareholders. Depending on the client’s needs, a sale can proceed through a full or partial sale, joint venture, spin-off or split-off.

Buy-Side Advisory

We assist shareholders in a successful acquisition at the most attractive possible valuation of the target. Depending on confidentiality needs and time-sensitivity, we pursue a negotiated acquisition, a multiple-round auction or an unsolicited approach.

Creditor Representation

We advise individual creditors and creditor groups with exposure to Latin American and Caribbean borrowers involved in restructuring, bankruptcy and other distressed situations. In many cases, we engage the borrower preemptively on behalf of creditors to maximize overall recovery.

  • Organize the formation of working creditor groups
  • Lead negotiations as the point of contact with the borrower and its advisors
  • Negotiate immediate forbearance agreements, waivers or amendments
  • Review short-term cash flow projections and perform a comprehensive debt sustainability analysis
  • Perform business and collateral valuations and analyze potential enhancements
  • Assess restructuring proposals against alternatives, including liquidation
  • Define a clear and efficient process strategy with legal counsel
  • Coordinate the process timeline and related documentation
  • Participate in all legal, exchange and solicitation processes required for closing
  • Monitor the borrower’s post-restructuring performance

Borrower Representation

We advise public and private sector borrowers facing liquidity challenges or capital structure issues, from voluntary out-of-court negotiations to Chapter 11 based processes, asset sale strategies and new capital raising, in all negotiations with lenders, bondholders, trade creditors and other stakeholders.

  • Review short-term cash flow projections and perform a comprehensive debt sustainability analysis
  • Lead negotiations as the point of contact with creditors and their advisors
  • Negotiate immediate forbearance agreements, waivers or amendments
  • Assess restructuring alternatives and make recommendations
  • Provide enterprise and collateral valuation analyses
  • Raise new capital, including debtor-in-possession financing
  • Advise on the negotiation, structuring and solicitation of exchange offers
  • Provide expert testimony as required under the relevant bankruptcy code

Debt Advisory

Our senior bankers advise and negotiate on behalf of public and private sector borrowers on refinancing existing debt and issuing new debt in regional and international markets, across the bank market, the bond market and specialized credit funds. We also advise on all aspects of the credit ratings process.

  • Undertake financial due diligence to confirm capital structure objectives
  • Evaluate secured and unsecured facilities, securitized financings and other structures
  • Lead negotiations with capital providers
  • Advise on structuring, documentation and transaction execution
