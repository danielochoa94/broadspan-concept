Rio de Janeiro, Brazil, March 2026. BroadSpan Capital acted as exclusive financial advisor to Ouro Negro Tecnologias em Equipamentos Industriais S.A. (“ouronova”) and its shareholders on an equity capital raise with an undisclosed international strategic investor.

The proceeds will support the scaling of ouronova’s commercial operations and the continued development of next-generation technologies for the offshore energy sector.

“We are very pleased to have supported ouronova in identifying an ideal strategic investor by leveraging our experience in the energy and oilfield services sector, and working through not only BroadSpan’s presence in the U.S. and Latin America, but also through our international partners around the world.”

Leonardo Antunes, Head of Brazil, BroadSpan Capital

Founded in 2010 and headquartered in Rio de Janeiro, ouronova develops monitoring, control and inspection solutions for the energy sector, with an increasing focus on energy diversification. Its open innovation model draws on National Petroleum Agency levy funds to finance projects in partnership with leading oil and gas players, and since 2012 it has filed more than 30 patents, 10 of them granted across Brazil and five other jurisdictions.

In 2024, ouronova launched an expansion plan to accelerate sales of its riser monitoring and robotic inspection portfolio while rolling out well completion, well intervention and well integrity solutions now in final development.
