The firm

Founded to bring world-class investment banking to Latin America’s middle market.

An independent firm of professionals who spent many years at major international financial institutions before dedicating their careers to emerging markets.

Who we are

Independent, senior-led and cross-border by design.

BroadSpan was founded in 2001 to address increasing demand for high-quality investment banking services among emerging middle-market companies in Latin America, the Caribbean and the U.S.

With offices in Miami, Mexico City, Medellín, Rio de Janeiro and São Paulo, along with collaborative agreements in selected markets, the BroadSpan team covers Latin America, the Caribbean and certain segments of the U.S. market across all major industries. In selected cases, BroadSpan has entered into formal strategic partnerships with global institutions to provide investment banking expertise regionally.

The firm’s advisory business is organized into two practice areas, Investment Banking Advisory and Financial Restructuring Advisory. Asset management activities are conducted through BroadSpan Asset Management LLC.
