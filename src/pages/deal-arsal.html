BroadSpan Advises on Sale of El Salvador’s Laboratorios Arsal to Costa Rica’s Calox

Client
Grupo Vargas
Country
El Salvador
Sector
Pharmaceuticals
Role
Exclusive financial advisor

San Salvador, El Salvador, November 2023. BroadSpan Capital acted as exclusive financial advisor to Grupo Vargas and its shareholders on the sale of 100% of the outstanding shares of Laboratorios Arsal, S.A. (“Arsal”) to Calox de Costa Rica, S.A. (“Calox”).

Arsal’s distributor network and dedicated sales force in 8 countries, along with several top products across key segments in El Salvador and other markets, give Calox an immediate opportunity to expand its reach.

“We are delighted to have been able to support the Vargas Group with this transaction, where we were able to leverage not only our long-standing focus on serving Central America’s leading private companies, but also our experience in the regional pharmaceutical industry.”

Mike Gerrard, CEO, BroadSpan Capital

Headquartered in San Salvador, Arsal is a leading pharmaceutical manufacturer with over 78 years of experience in Central America and the Caribbean. It was acquired in 2016 by Grupo Vargas, a leading regional pharmaceutical player.

Calox, based in San José, Costa Rica, is a pharmaceutical manufacturer with 85 years in the industry and a presence in Central America and the Dominican Republic.
