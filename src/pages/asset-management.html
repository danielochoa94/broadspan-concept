Who We Are

BroadSpan Asset Management (BSAM) is the asset management arm of BroadSpan, an independent financial group focused on Latin America and the Caribbean since 2001.

Established in 2004 and headquartered in Miami, BSAM manages dedicated strategies for institutional investors, built on rigorous fundamental analysis, disciplined risk management and long-standing relationships across the region.
