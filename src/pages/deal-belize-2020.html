August 2020. BroadSpan Capital acted as exclusive financial advisor to the Belize Creditors’ Committee in the modification of terms of Belize’s U.S. Dollar Bonds due 2034.

After the Government of Belize announced liquidity challenges associated with the COVID-19 pandemic on June 17, 2020, the committee’s steering members engaged constructively with the authorities. On July 17, the Government launched a consent solicitation seeking bondholder approval to capitalize the coupons due August 20 and November 20, 2020 and February 20, 2021.

The solicitation added an information covenant giving investors quarterly calls with the authorities on economic developments and debt projections. Recognizing the importance of reforms to diversify Belize’s economy and build resilience against external shocks, the committee announced its intention to assent. Orrick, Herrington & Sutcliffe LLP acted as the committee’s legal advisor.
