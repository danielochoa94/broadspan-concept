March 2022. BroadSpan Capital acted as exclusive financial advisor to the UBS Puerto Rico Family of Funds with respect to the restructuring of the debt of the Employees Retirement System of the Government of Puerto Rico (“ERS”).

The Funds, together with other coordinating bondholders, held over 71%, or US$2.1 billion, of the US$3.0 billion in eligible bond claims prior to the restructuring.
