November 2015. BroadSpan Capital acted as exclusive financial advisor to the Steering Committee of Grenada Bondholders in the restructuring of Grenada’s sovereign debt.

The committee was formed to address Grenada’s default on its U.S. Dollar and E.C. Dollar Bonds due 2025. In April 2015, it confirmed an agreement in principle on key financial terms: an initial 25% principal reduction, a further 25% reduction upon timely completion of the Sixth Review of Grenada’s IMF program, a 15-year maturity with equal amortization from the second year, a 7% coupon, and full capitalization of past-due and accrued interest. Bondholders would also share in a portion of certain revenues from Grenada’s Citizenship by Investment Program.

The committee commended Grenada’s commitment to a comprehensive and collaborative restructuring, and the constructive creditor engagement that brought the parties to agreement.
