About BSAM

Regional Platform. Institutional Standards.

Our Story

BroadSpan Asset Management was established in 2004 as the asset management arm of BroadSpan, an independent investment banking and asset management group founded in Miami in 2001 to serve Latin America and the Caribbean.

For more than two decades the BroadSpan group has advised corporations, investors and governments on cross-border mergers and acquisitions, capital raising and financial restructurings across the region.

BSAM brings that knowledge of companies, issuers and markets to the management of dedicated investment strategies for institutional investors.

Our Approach

Fundamental analysis

Bottom-up research on every company, issuer and transaction.

Relative value

A clear view of value across markets, asset classes and the capital structure.

Risk discipline

Risk controls tailored to each strategy’s objectives.

Alignment

Structures and incentives that put investors’ interests first.

Transparency

Clear terms and consistent, institutional-quality reporting.

Part of BroadSpan

Through BroadSpan Capital, BSAM draws on offices and affiliates in Miami, Rio de Janeiro, São Paulo, Medellín and Mexico City and on BroadSpan’s role as the exclusive Latin American representative of Mergers Alliance (excluding Mexico).
