March 2017. BroadSpan Capital acted as exclusive financial advisor to the Belize Creditors’ Committee in the restructuring of Belize’s sovereign debt.

Key holders of Belize’s U.S. Dollar Bonds due 2038 formed the committee in November 2016, after the Government of Belize announced it would seek to open discussions with bondholders. The committee organized to evaluate the Government’s statements on its situation, facilitate communication among bondholders and pursue appropriate actions, noting that the bonds stemmed from a 2013 restructuring that had already provided over US$100 million in debt service relief.

The committee called for engagement consistent with the G20-endorsed Principles for Stable Capital Flows and Fair Debt Restructuring, including transparency, open dialogue, good faith negotiations and fair treatment among creditor classes. BroadSpan advised the committee alongside Blitzer Consulting as special advisor.
