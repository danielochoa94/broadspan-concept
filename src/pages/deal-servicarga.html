BroadSpan Advises Brazil’s Servicarga on Sale to VIX Logística

Rio de Janeiro, Brazil, March 2022. BroadSpan Capital acted as exclusive financial advisor to Servicarga and its shareholders on its sale to VIX Logística S/A.

The acquisition supports VIX Logística’s growth strategy, expanding it into automotive logistics and adding a new client portfolio in a segment it did not previously serve.

Servicarga, headquartered in Caxias do Sul, Rio Grande do Sul, specializes in logistics and transportation for trucks, buses, utility vehicles, parts and components, along with special and heavy transport, driver services, and chassis and truck reassembly.

VIX Logística, headquartered in Vitória, Espírito Santo, is controlled by Grupo Águia Branca and the International Finance Corporation (IFC). Its activities span dedicated logistics, fleet outsourcing and management, cargo handling, automotive, land and international logistics, and chartering, and it sells light and heavy used vehicles in Brazil.
