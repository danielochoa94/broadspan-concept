August 2020. BroadSpan Capital acted as co-financial advisor, alongside UBS Investment Bank, to the Ecuador Steering Committee in the restructuring of the Republic of Ecuador’s sovereign debt.

Large institutional holders of Ecuador’s international bonds organized the committee in June 2020 to engage with the authorities on a potential re-profiling of the country’s external debt. Representative of Ecuador’s bondholder base, with holdings across each series of bonds, the committee sought a constructive dialogue that would protect the interests of all holders.

Sensitive to the challenges Ecuador faced, the committee welcomed the authorities’ commitment to transparency, good faith negotiations and fair treatment among eligible creditor classes, and committed to engage in line with the G20-endorsed Principles for Stable Capital Flows and Fair Debt Restructuring.
