Responsible Investing

ESG as Risk Management.
Impact as Value Creation.

We believe that sound environmental, social and governance practices protect value, and that capital directed to underserved markets can create lasting economic and social benefits. ESG considerations are part of our investment process across strategies.

Our Commitments

  1. Environmental and social risk assessment as part of investment due diligence

  2. An environmental and social management system aligned with [IFC Performance Standards], including an exclusion list

  3. Governance, transparency and accountability in every investment

  4. Ongoing monitoring and regular reporting to investors
