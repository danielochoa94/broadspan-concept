BroadSpan Advises Court-Appointed Liquidators on Sale of COLFIRE

CL Financial liquidators
Country
Trinidad & Tobago
Sector

Role
Exclusive financial advisor

Port of Spain, Trinidad, February 2023. BroadSpan Capital acted as exclusive financial advisor to the court-appointed liquidators of CL Financial Limited, David Holukoff and Hugh Dickson of Grant Thornton, on the sale of 94.24% of the outstanding shares of Colonial Fire & General Insurance Company Limited (“COLFIRE”) to Trinidad and Tobago Insurance Limited (“TATIL”), whose ultimate parent is ANSA McAL Limited.

The sale was overseen by the High Court of Justice of Trinidad and Tobago and required a public offer and take-over bid for all issued and outstanding ordinary shares. On February 6, 2023, TATIL initiated the process to take up and pay for 15,357,405 ordinary shares, representing 97.5% of the total shareholding.

With holders of more than 90% of the shares accepting the offer, TATIL may compulsorily acquire the remaining shares under Section 202 of the Companies Act of Trinidad and Tobago.

COLFIRE is a leading motor, property and general insurer that has operated in Trinidad and Tobago for over 60 years.
