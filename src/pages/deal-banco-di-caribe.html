BroadSpan Advises the Central Bank of Curaçao and Sint Maarten on the Sale of Banco di Caribe

Client
Central Bank of Curaçao and Sint Maarten
Country
Curaçao
Sector
Banking
Role
Exclusive financial advisor

Willemstad, Curaçao, June 2022. BroadSpan Capital acted as exclusive financial advisor to the Central Bank of Curaçao and Sint Maarten (“CBCS”) in the sale of 100% of the equity shares of Banco di Caribe by Ennia Caribe Holding NV.

The sale includes all of Banco di Caribe’s banking activities in Curaçao, Aruba, Bonaire and Sint Maarten, together with the insurance and investment activities of Van der Lubbe Assurantiën NV, Bancarib Real Insurance Curaçao NV and BdC Investments BV.

The buyer, United Group Holdings, is a financial services provider active in Curaçao and in European countries including the Netherlands, the United Kingdom and Luxembourg.
